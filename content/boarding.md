## Boarding

![Picture](/images/7/5/8/2/75820791/boarding_1_orig.jpg)

We have 12 duplex condos for our boarding guests. Each cat has their own private duplex with a pass-through, so their litter box stays on one side while their food, water, and bedding are comfortably arranged on the other.

Our staff takes genuine pride in caring for every guest. We take the time to get to know each cat's personality and preferences, so your companion feels as settled and loved as possible while you are away.
