## Geriatric Medicine

As your cat ages, their needs change — and so does our approach to their care. Senior cats generally benefit from more frequent wellness exams and lab work, which allow us to catch age-related conditions such as kidney disease, hyperthyroidism, arthritis, and diabetes early, when they are most manageable.

We have extensive experience with older cats and take the time to understand each patient's history and comfort level. Our goal is to help your senior companion live as comfortably and fully as possible for as long as possible.
