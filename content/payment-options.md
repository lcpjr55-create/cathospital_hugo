## Payment Options  
  
  
![Payment Options](/images/7/5/8/2/75820791/unnamed-2_orig.jpg)

So that we may continue to provide exceptional care to all of our clients, payment is expected at the time services are rendered. We accept Visa, Mastercard, Discover, and American Express, as well as personal checks and cash. We also accept [CareCredit](http://www.carecredit.com/), a third-party financing option for veterinary expenses.

We will provide estimates for all surgical procedures and most treatment plans so there are no surprises. If you have not received an estimate for your visit and would like one, please just ask — we are happy to help.
